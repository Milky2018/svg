## Description
The 0.4.0 public-surface cleanup left `Transform::identity` and
`Transform::is_identity` public even though ISS-037 classifies identity-matrix
and renderer optimization helpers as internal implementation details.

## Design
Remove public visibility from the two helpers without changing their internal
behavior. Keep authored transform construction through `parse_transform` and
the retained transform operations. Do not expand this correction into a wider
redesign of the `Transform` API.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] `Transform::identity` and `Transform::is_identity` are absent from the generated public interface.
- [x] Internal parser, renderer, and white-box test uses continue to compile and pass.
- [x] All repository validation gates pass.
