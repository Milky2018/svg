## Description
Replace the early-integer SVG rasterizer with the deterministic continuous-geometry, coverage, paint, and compositing pipeline accepted in ADR-0001 through ADR-0008.

## Design
Deliver the renderer as dependency-ordered, green stages. Preserve the high-level rendering facade, remove obsolete low-level rendering APIs at cutover, and keep external resource fetching, browser pixel parity, font shaping, and incremental rendering outside the epic.
