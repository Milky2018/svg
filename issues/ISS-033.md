## Description
Prepare and sample gradients, patterns, and resolved raster images through the common coverage and compositing pipeline.

## Design
Evaluate gradient stops directly in their computed interpolation space. Render bounded affine-aware Pattern Tiles through the complete scene renderer. Sample images and patterns in premultiplied working colors with the specified nearest, bilinear, or bicubic filter.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Linear and radial gradients cover units, transforms, href inheritance, spread modes, focal geometry, repeated stops, and degeneracy.
- [ ] Patterns cover both unit systems, view boxes, transforms, recursive content, wrap-safe sampling, limits, and per-render caching.
- [ ] Raster images cover complete affine placement, viewport Coverage, preserveAspectRatio, filtering, opacity, and resolver failure diagnostics.
- [ ] PNG/JPEG decoding and output encoding remain host responsibilities and are documented.
