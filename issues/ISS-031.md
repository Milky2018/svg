## Description
Replace early integer polygons and shape-specific raster routines with Segment Paths, device-adaptive approximation, and deterministic fixed-point cell Coverage.

## Design
Canonicalize every basic shape into Segment Paths. Preserve curves and arcs until device-space approximation, then use 16.16 fixed-point edges with Int64 intermediates for nonzero and even-odd Coverage.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Rectangles, rounded rectangles, circles, ellipses, lines, polygons, polylines, and paths share one coverage pipeline.
- [ ] Device-space curve error is bounded to one eighth pixel with degenerate-recursion guards.
- [ ] Symmetric fractional translations produce symmetric numeric coverage evidence.
- [ ] Nonzero, even-odd, affine, skew, and degenerate geometry tests pass on every target.
